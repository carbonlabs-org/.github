# Carbon Labs

### Make a difference.

Carbon Labs is an **independent, multidisciplinary laboratory** that explores ideas, builds useful things, and develops much of its work in the open.

We are not limited to a single field. Our work can span **software, hardware, artificial intelligence, creative technology, human-computer interaction, art, and scientific or philosophical inquiry**.

What connects these projects is not the technology they use, but the intention behind them:

> **To explore worthwhile ideas and turn them into something real.**

A project at Carbon might begin as a question, an experiment, or a problem worth solving. It may grow into an open-source tool, a prototype, research, a creative work, or a complete product.

Not every experiment has to succeed. Discovering what doesn't work can be valuable too.

We develop much of our work openly so that others can **use it, study it, question it, improve it, and build alongside us**.

Carbon Labs is still at the beginning. We want it to become a place where people from different disciplines can bring their abilities together, experiment freely, and build ideas worth pursuing.

---

## Build With Us

Carbon Labs is open to people who want to **build, experiment, research, design, question, or contribute**.

If one of our projects interests you, explore the repository, open an issue, propose an idea, improve something, or contribute directly.

You don't have to belong to one particular discipline. If you can contribute something meaningful, there is a place to start.
